FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference in availability between the two products?

Kudzu Root Starch is fully dried and shelf-stable, so it's available year-round. Canh Mandarin Oranges are fresh fruit, harvested only in season (roughly November to January, lunar calendar).

Do you ship outside Vietnam?

Domestic orders ship nationwide within Vietnam. For international/export orders (Kudzu Root Starch only), please reach out via email or Zalo to discuss shipping method, quantity, and packaging.

Is your production certified?

Canh Mandarin Oranges are VietGAP certified (certificate No. 112/CN-TĐC-TT-20-0012). Kudzu Root Starch is made at household scale and does not yet hold a formal certification — we say this openly.

Can I order wholesale or in bulk?

Yes, we welcome wholesale and export inquiries for Kudzu Root Starch — message us on Zalo or email to discuss quantity, pricing, and packaging.
