About Us

A family farm, made with care

Nong San Nha Lam started from a simple wish: to keep our family's traditional way of making kudzu root starch and growing Canh mandarin oranges alive. Each batch of starch is hand-filtered and then dried in our own dedicated drying kiln — no bleaching agents added. Our oranges are grown at our own family orchard, left to ripen naturally on the tree. We are a small household producer: we make what we can properly make, and we prioritize real quality over volume.

Our Canh mandarin oranges are grown by a family member of the Nhung Giang Fruit Tree Cooperative (Lang Son province), and are VietGAP certified (certificate No. 112/CN-TĐC-TT-20-0012, valid until 22 Dec 2028). Our kudzu root starch, made at household scale, does not yet hold a formal certification — we say this openly so that customers, especially wholesale and export partners, understand our current scale and can discuss the right way to work together.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does the kudzu root starch keep?

It's fully dried, so it keeps well — store in a cool, dry place away from moisture. Available year-round; we always have fresh stock ready to ship.

Do you ship outside Vietnam?

Domestic orders ship nationwide within Vietnam. For international/export orders (kudzu root starch only), please reach out via Zalo or the export section below to discuss shipping method, quantity, and packaging.

What if my order arrives damaged or wrong?

As this is a food product, we don't accept returns on opened or used items. We'll replace the item or refund if it arrives damaged, the wrong item/quantity was sent, or there's a production defect — film an unboxing video and message us on Zalo within 24 hours of delivery.

When are Canh mandarin oranges in season?

Harvest season is roughly November to January (lunar calendar) only. Outside the season you can still reserve ahead — message us on Zalo and we'll notify you first when the new harvest is ready.

Is your production certified?

Our Canh mandarin oranges are VietGAP certified (certificate No. 112/CN-TĐC-TT-20-0012). Our kudzu root starch is made at household scale and does not yet hold a formal certification — we say this openly. No additives or bleaching agents either way.

Do you handle wholesale or export orders?

Yes, for kudzu root starch — see the Wholesale & Export section above, or message us on Zalo to discuss quantity, pricing, and packaging.
