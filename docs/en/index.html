About Us

A family farm, made with care

Nong San Nha Lam started from a simple wish: to keep our family's traditional way of making arrowroot (kudzu) starch and growing Canh mandarin oranges alive. Each batch of starch is hand-filtered and sun-dried the traditional way — no bleaching agents, no industrial dryers. Our oranges are grown at our own family orchard, left to ripen naturally on the tree. We are a small household producer: we make what we can properly make, and we prioritize real quality over volume.

Our Canh mandarin oranges are grown by a family member of the Nhung Giang Fruit Tree Cooperative (Lang Son province), and are VietGAP certified (certificate No. 112/CN-TĐC-TT-20-0012, valid until 22 Dec 2028). Our arrowroot starch, made at household scale, does not yet hold a formal certification — we say this openly so that customers, especially wholesale and export partners, understand our current scale and can discuss the right way to work together.
